=== THE BRIEF ===

A 9:16, ~60s promo for creators/influencers planning their next collaboration
video. It follows one real job end to end: drop the transcript of the last
collab into Prism; Prism splits it into the stages that job needs — research,
reasoning, writing, images — and runs each in the creator's own signed-in
Chrome, passing every stage's output forward as context to the next.

The beats the user asked for, in order: insert the old transcript · do the
research · script the next collab · make the thumbnails.

=== WHAT PRISM ACTUALLY IS ===

Prism takes one plain-language task, uses a Groq routing brain (LLaMA 3.3-70B)
to split it into targeted prompts, then drives the user's own logged-in Chrome
through the specialist AIs they chose — research, reasoning, writing, images,
video/audio, app-building, decks — passing each stage's output forward as
context to the next. No passwords stored; it reuses existing Chrome sessions.
25 tools across 7 categories, plus /remote (send tasks from your phone) and
/attach (any file into the pipeline).

It has no engine of its own: the GUI imports the same core the CLI uses, and
both read and write the same ~/.prism/config.json.

=== THE SEVEN STAGES, IN THE APP'S OWN WORDS ===

Look things up      Find the facts and sources this needs
Think it through    Work out the angle and the argument
Write it up         Turn the thinking into clear words
Make the images     Generate the artwork to go with it
Make the video      Produce the video or audio piece
Build the tool      Stand up the app or page itself
Build the slides    A clean deck, ready to present

Each row carries a tool chip you can change. The router's suggestion is starred
and tagged "Suggested"; a tool you explicitly named in the task is pre-selected
and tagged "You picked this". Click a row to drop that step. Then: Start the
work.

=== BRAND ===

Register: brand. Voice: plain and confident, never hypey — it explains a real
mechanism (light through a prism) rather than promising magic. Three words:
refractive, terminal-native, unpretentious. It should feel like discovering a
sharp tool, not being sold a platform.

The prism is literal, not decorative: one white prompt refracts into exactly
the spectrum bands (categories) the user picks. Colour = meaning; each of the
7 categories owns a band of the spectrum.

Show the real thing. Real frames, the real config format, real commands. No
fake controls, no invented features.

=== ANTI-REFERENCES (things this video must not become) ===

- SaaS-cream landing pages with a hero-metric template and identical icon-card
  feature grids.
- "One AI does everything" magic-box marketing. Prism's honesty is that it
  routes to *other people's* tools and drives your *own* browser.
- Generic dark "terminal dev-tool" costume with mono-everything and no idea
  underneath. The differentiator is the prism/spectrum concept, not the
  aesthetic.

=== ACCESSIBILITY ===

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text >= 4.5:1 on the near-black canvas. Spectrum accents used
for state or meaning are always paired with a label or shape, never colour
alone.
