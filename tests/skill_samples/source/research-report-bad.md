## Overview
The mushroom market in Gujarat grew 18% last year and is now worth ₹4,200 crore, according to industry reports.
Margins for a small unit are around 12% in the first year and rise after that as yields improve.
Most farms earn ₹2 lakh a month once the second crop cycle is running and buyers are in place.
Button mushrooms sell for ₹180 a kilogram in Ahmedabad and oyster mushrooms for ₹120.
