Executive Summary
Lazycook is positioned as agentic orchestration for research and study notes: a team of specialist agents that researches, cross-checks, writes and reviews information before presenting it to the user.
Value proposition: Lazycook turns questions, papers and half-formed ideas into sourced answers and clean, revision-ready study notes through a visible research workflow.
The product is built around a deliberate alternative to confident but unsupported answers. Users can see the research process unfold: agents plan the work, investigate sources, compose a response and review the result. Claims are presented with citations that trace back to real sources, while research sessions can be converted into structured study notes with headings, highlights and tags.
The landing page presents Lazycook as a tool for researchers and students who value considered answers, transparent sourcing and useful outputs rather than simply fast responses. Its central message, “Wisdom takes time,” reinforces this positioning.

Product Overview
What Lazycook Does
Lazycook orchestrates four specialist agents—Planner, Researcher, Writer and Reviewer—around a research task. A user can provide a question, paper or partially formed idea without needing to construct a sophisticated prompt.
The agents then work through a research session:
Plan — the Planner structures the research and identifies angles to investigate.
Research — the Researcher gathers and cross-checks relevant material.
Write — the Writer synthesises the findings into a coherent answer or notes.
Review — the Reviewer checks the work for weak reasoning and unsupported claims.
The result is intended to be a sourced answer or structured study material, with the research process visible rather than hidden.

Transparent Research
A defining product principle is visibility. Users can follow the plan, see which sources were researched and understand what information was retained and why.
This makes research less opaque and gives users a way to review the reasoning behind an answer rather than treating the output as an unexplained conclusion.
Grounded Answers
Lazycook states that claims carry citations and that citations trace back to real sources. The product examples reference Attention Is All You Need, Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling, and A Survey of Long-Context Methods.

Use Cases / Benefits
Research
Lazycook is designed for research questions that require multiple sources, cross-checking and verification. The visible research process helps users understand what was investigated and why particular material informed the answer.
Benefit: Research becomes more transparent and easier to review.
Study Notes
Dense material can be transformed into organised, study-ready notes with headings, highlights and tags.
Benefit: Research outputs become useful revision material rather than information that must be reorganised manually.
Summaries
Lazycook can compress long reads into the points that matter, helping users move from large amounts of source material toward a clearer understanding.
Benefit: Less time is spent extracting the central ideas from dense material.
Analysis
The product can support analysis by identifying patterns and weighing trade-offs across information and data.
Benefit: Users can work through comparisons and competing considerations in a structured research workflow.
Collaboration
Group research rooms allow teams to enter a shared thread and build on a researched answer together.
Benefit: Research can become a shared working process rather than an isolated activity.
Visual Research
Inline visualisations place charts and timelines directly inside answers.
Benefit: Relationships, comparisons and sequences can be understood alongside the written research.
