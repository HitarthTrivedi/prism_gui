⚠️ DRAWING-SCOPE WARNING
The attached DWG is not a civil/site survey or CCTV services layout. It is a General Arrangement (GA) / mechanical equipment drawing for an OBMS assembly. The layer names and geometry predominantly represent fabricated mechanical components such as channels, plates, shafts, rollers, bearings, bolts, bushes, belts and the magnet body.
Therefore, CCTV, camera, cable, conduit, fibre, gate and perimeter quantities must NOT be derived from this drawing. Doing so would fabricate quantities that are not supported by the drawing.
The quantities below are therefore presented as a mechanical BOQ/BOM take-off from the supplied GA drawing data. Rates and amounts are intentionally left blank. Material grades, thicknesses, weights and fabrication details should be independently verified against the approved manufacturing BOM/detail drawings before procurement or tendering.

Important quantity-control note
The supplied quantities are CAD geometry measurements. They are authoritative for this take-off as instructed, but they are not equivalent to final procurement counts.
For example, UC_212-BEARING = 9,996.91 in represents accumulated CAD geometry on that layer; it does not mean 9,996.91 inches of bearing should be purchased. Likewise, the 7,010.02-in PIPE_FOR_ROLLER layer represents geometry and must be decomposed into actual roller/pipe pieces before procurement.
Final procurement BOM should therefore be generated from the individual component/detail drawings, with piece count, dimensions, material grade, thickness/diameter, weight and finish added.
