Climate Suitability (Gujarat)
Gujarat has four mushroom-relevant climate zones: arid northern belt (Banaskantha, Patan, Mahesana), central Gujarat plain (Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Anand), south Gujarat coastal belt (Surat, Navsari, Valsad), and Saurashtra peninsula (Rajkot, Jamnagar).

Button mushroom (Agaricus bisporus) requires 22–26°C for spawn run and 14–18°C for fruiting with 80–90% relative humidity.
